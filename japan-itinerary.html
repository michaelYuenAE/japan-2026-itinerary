Your saved places, shaping the trip

Your saved places point to an atmospheric mix of onsen, art, odd local culture and old streets. Izu is now in the main route; Oita and Niigata/Sado stay as strong alternatives for another trip.

Osaka & Kansai

Festival energy, cafés, Mount Shigi, Wakayama and the cat train.

Kanazawa

Tea houses, crafts and a calmer kind of traditional city.

Izu / Shuzenji

The best Hakone follow-on: riverside ryokan, bamboo and a much quieter onsen town.

Oita / Yufuin

Your saved onsen-and-cinematic alternative: rural scenery, ryokan and anime-adjacent finds.

Sado & Niigata

Kitazawa ruins, Echigo-Tsumari, Kiyotsu Gorge and sake—save this as an art-and-landscape trip.

Wakayama

A playful final detour: themed trains, cat stationmaster and countryside calm.

Booking rule: on any weekday transfer, arrive by mid-afternoon. For ryokan stays, confirm a real work table, stable Wi-Fi, and a dinner time before your 18:00 Japan meeting.
